# Timeline & trips

## Purpose

Give travel-heavy professionals a chronological narrative of work movement and attach evidence to trips/legs.

## Views

- FY timeline (months → days with evidence markers)
- Trips list + trip detail (legs, linked evidence)

## Behaviours

- Create trip manually; AI may suggest trips from flight evidence later
- Attach/detach evidence
- Purpose: work / mixed / personal (affects deduction hints, not calculations)

## Acceptance

- Landscape tablet/desktop show richer timeline
- Phone uses vertical agenda pattern
