# Income & payslips

## Purpose

Organise employment income evidence (payslips, allowances) without becoming payroll software.

## Behaviours

- Employers list (CRUD)
- Payslips linked to evidence files
- FY summary of captured payslips (counts, date coverage gaps) — **not** tax payable

## Acceptance

- Gap hints (“No payslip for March”) feed readiness
- Manual entry of totals allowed when OCR fails
