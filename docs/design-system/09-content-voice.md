# Content & voice

## Voice

Clear, confident, human. Short sentences. No bureaucratic tax jargon in chrome.

## Examples

| Prefer | Avoid |
|--------|-------|
| “Captured. We’ll organise it.” | “Document uploaded successfully to DMS.” |
| “Needs a quick look” | “EXCEPTION: CLASSIFICATION CONFIDENCE BELOW THRESHOLD” |
| “Return readiness” | “Compliance completion metric” |

## Disclaimers

Present once where relevant (deductions, readiness): AJX Tax organises evidence and maintains a traceable tax position. It does not provide tax advice or lodge your return.

## Empty states

Aspirational and actionable — illustration, one sentence, one CTA.

## Errors

Plain language + next step. Never expose stack traces, HTTP codes, or function names.

| Prefer | Avoid |
|--------|-------|
| “We couldn’t upload that file. Check your connection and try again.” | `StorageError: putObject failed 503` |
| “This PDF is password-protected. Upload an unlocked copy.” | `PDF_ENCRYPTED` |

## AI & numbers

- Always pair suggestions with confidence and a short “why”
- Label estimates as indicative; never imply lodged tax truth
- Offer “Source” when showing calculated figures

## Priorities

Simplicity · Clarity · Reliability · Trust — see [Standards](../standards/00-overview.md).
